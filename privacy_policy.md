# Privacy Policy — Serpentine Performance Tester

**Last updated:** May 17, 2026

## 1. Overview

Serpentine Performance Tester ("the App") is developed by an individual developer. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device
The App stores the following data **only on your device** in local JSON files:
- **Test results** — scores and metrics from completed performance tests, used to display your history on next launch.
- **Trial and subscription status** — to track your free trial period and premium activation.

This data **never leaves your device** and is not transmitted to any server.

### 2.2 Global Rating
If you choose to submit your score to the **Global Rating**, only your **test score** (a numeric value) is submitted. No name, email, device ID, or any personally identifiable information is collected or associated with this score.

### 2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect your name, email, or contact information.
- We do not collect location data.
- We do not collect camera or microphone data.
- We do not use analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs.
- We do not share any data with third parties.

---

## 3. Device Sensors

The App uses device sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope) solely to perform performance tests. Sensor data is processed in real time **on your device only** and is never stored or transmitted.

---

## 4. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional premium subscription (monthly) and a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is processed through the app store platform (Huawei AppGallery / Samsung Galaxy Store). We do not receive or store your payment details.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
